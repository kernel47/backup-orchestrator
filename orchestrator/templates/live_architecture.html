Connection direction

The worker initiates HTTP requests to the master: heartbeat, claim, lease renewal and result submission. No inbound listener is required on a worker.

Local time, fixed period

Daily schedules use the satellite timezone. The assigned period is passed in UTC and stays unchanged for retries. The worker measures execution start, finish and duration.

Reported outcome

The collector owns collection and delivery decisions. Tasker uses its final status and also detects technical failures such as a timeout, invalid JSON or a failed process.

Connected workers & configuration

Registration from the master, machine information from worker heartbeats. No environment secrets are transmitted.
